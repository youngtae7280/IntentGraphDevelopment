## Consolidation Decision

The helper stops at repeated mechanics. It does not own probe definitions, semantic baseline scopes, report boundary fields, or phase-specific claims. Those remain in the individual harness scripts:

- `tools/run_cf0_delta_negative_probes.py` remains the historical P1.3 additive-delta harness.
- `tools/run_cf0_overlay_contract_negative_probes.py` remains the historical P1.9 unsupported-operation overlay contract harness.
- `tools/run_cf0_input_validation_negative_probes.py` remains the current P1.11 invalid-integer overlay contract harness.

This keeps the reports readable while reducing the risk that future harnesses drift in subprocess invocation, expected-failure matching, or baseline rerun behavior.
