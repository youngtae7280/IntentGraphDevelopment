# P9.34.R Deep Inspection Rendering Review

## Decision

The renderer revision is ready for user visual review. Automated checks prove the camera,
screen-space selection, cached material, interaction, and performance contracts. The
subjective premium-material judgment remains a user review concern.

## Result

- `100x` remains a one-click inspection level.
- the real camera maximum is `256x`;
- renderer zoom, logical zoom, and effective geometry zoom reach `256`;
- maximum-zoom selected relation width is `0.30px` at `0.42` opacity;
- maximum-zoom virtual geometry scale remains `1`;
- the active material is `cached-celestial-ceramic-v4`;
- the material sprite cache remains bounded to 96 entries;
- deep-zoom material candidates remain viewport-local;
- graph facts, relations, and product workflow records are unchanged.

## Evidence

The headless browser probe uses the real generated WindowsUtility Workbench at
1440 by 1000 pixels. It checks loaded graph counts, canvas pixels, maximum camera state,
selected relation screen width, selected endpoint material variation, inspector content,
and runtime errors. The page probe clicks the real `100x`, `256x`, and zoom-out controls,
pans at maximum zoom, restores the camera, and enforces bounded synchronous navigation
handler times. A fail-closed negative harness mutates each required observation.
The external runner also measures the complete browser capture against a 45-second wall
clock ceiling. Viewport material candidates must remain below the larger of 256 nodes or
10 percent of the graph, rather than merely below the total node count.

## Residual Risk

Canvas pixel variation can prove that the material exists and contains optical detail; it
cannot prove that a person considers the finish refined. The final visual judgment is made
against the live Workbench, not the material profile name alone.
