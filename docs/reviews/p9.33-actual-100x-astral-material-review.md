# P9.33 Actual 100x Astral Material Review

Status: pass for the bounded Workbench rendering refinement.

## Decision

P9.33 passes. The WindowsUtility Workbench now uses an actual Cytoscape camera zoom of
`100x` instead of stopping at renderer `24x` and expanding graph coordinates. Maximum
zoom relation selection is quieter, and the cached node material has been replaced by
a darker faceted metal-glass treatment.

## Visual Result

- the maximum readout, renderer zoom, and effective geometry zoom all reach `100`
- virtual geometry remains at scale `1`, so the camera performs the zoom
- a selected relation stays thin but visible at `0.55px` and `0.68` opacity at maximum zoom
- unrelated semantic and stage-delta emphasis is suppressed while an element is selected
- default circular tokens are replaced by faceted, hexagonal, diamond, and squircle
  silhouettes selected by node kind
- `cached-astral-forged-glass-v3` uses a near-black forged core, asymmetric specular
  sweep, clipped facets, fine dual rims, limited ion color, and restrained halo
- sprite generation remains cached and viewport-local; a 120-model-unit spatial index
  limits deep-zoom material work to viewport candidates and the cache is capped at 96
- node-centered pixel evidence requires opaque, chromatic, color-bucket, and luminance
  thresholds instead of accepting only a material name and any nonzero pixel

## Browser Observation

Microsoft Edge `150.0.4078.65` loaded 8,208 nodes and 8,023 relations in one graph
instance. At maximum zoom the page reported renderer, logical, and effective zoom
`100`, virtual geometry scale `1`, selected relation width `0.55px`, opacity `0.68`,
and active material `cached-astral-forged-glass-v3`. The graph and material canvases
were nonblank and the selection inspector contained both endpoints.

The 1440 by 1000 screenshot and a node-centered material crop are validated separately.
The crop must retain at least 120 opaque pixels, 12 chromatic pixels, 8 quantized color
buckets, and a luminance range of 35. The observed crop recorded 195 opaque pixels,
162 chromatic pixels, 89 buckets, and luminance range 217. At x100 only 8 viewport
candidates were examined and 8,200 offscreen nodes were skipped. Browser runtime errors
were empty.
