## Scope

P1.17 adds a deterministic current-state coverage report for the CF0 code-first overlay. It does not add behavior, change source bytes, broaden the extractor, change B0 generated-code behavior, introduce dependencies, or claim a generic coverage framework.

The purpose is to make visible how the current extracted code facts are covered by Intent Units, mapping obligations, behavior verification, evidence, authority, and semantic history.

## P2/P3 Decisions

- `P1.17-P3-001`: structural low-level facts are not elevated to standalone Intent Units. Decision: intentional. CF0 should not model every AST-level fact as a development semantic unit; behavior-relevant coverage is the current bar.

## Recommendation

Continue. A reasonable next slice is a small current-state dashboard/workbench projection for CF0 coverage and history, or a focused cleanup that makes phase-entry roadmap status less manual. Do not open a broader extractor, UI, or benchmark without a separate Coordinator decision.
