## Decision

The deep-zoom and material correction passes its deterministic, browser, and server
gates and is ready for user visual review.

## Verified Result

- actual camera, logical zoom, and effective geometry zoom reach `512`;
- direct `100x`, `256x`, and `512x` controls are present;
- maximum selected-relation width is `0.08px` at `0.12` opacity;
- relation endpoints are rendered at 72% of normal precision material size and use
  compact endpoint ticks instead of full selected-node halos;
- real endpoint opaque bounds measure `12x14px` at maximum zoom against a `22x22px`
  fail-closed ceiling;
- active material identity is `cached-nebula-black-metal-v7`;
- selected semantic nodes use the tighter visible-body crop while relation endpoints
  remain compact;
- the material uses dark alloy facets, etched contours, sparse grain, and a thin
  asymmetric diffraction rim rather than stacked glossy borders;
- deep precision views remove the rectangular grid;
- the cache remains bounded to 96 sprites and rendering remains viewport-local;
- all 8,194 nodes and 7,986 relations remain loaded;
- graph facts, graph relations, layout coordinates, source, workflow, evidence,
  authority, history, delta, and code-diff domains are unchanged.
